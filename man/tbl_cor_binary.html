Table of the effect sizes of binary variables

Description

Formats the output of cor_binary_effects(): groups and sizes, means (SD), difference in means with its CI and t-test, rank-biserial correlation r_rb with its CI and Mann-Whitney test, and the observed r_s next to its maximum for this split, so that the attenuation of the correlation is visible.

Usage

tbl_cor_binary(eff, labels = NULL, digits = 2, title = NULL, subtitle = NULL)

Arguments

eff output of cor_binary_effects()
labels named vector variable -> label
digits decimals
title, subtitle override the automatic texts

Value

a gt_tbl

Examples

library("corrkit")

d <- simulate_cor_data(150)
tbl_cor_binary(cor_binary_effects(d, c("BIN_CRP", "BIN_IL6"), c("depression", "fatigue")),
               labels = attr(d, "labels"))

M₁ − M₀: difference in means, Student's t-test with equal variances (= test of the point-biserial r); 95% CI from stats::t.test() {stats}.
r_rb = 2U / (n₀ n₁) − 1 = P(Y₁ > Y₀) − P(Y₁ < Y₀) = 2 AUC − 1, U = Mann-Whitney statistic of stats::wilcox.test() {stats} (Kerby 2014, doi:10.2466/11.IT.3.1); 95% CI from effectsize::rank_biserial() {effectsize}.
Observed rₛ / max |rₛ|: Spearman correlation with the 0/1 variable and its maximum √(3pq) for this split (no ties): the correlation is bounded by the split and must not be compared with that of a continuous variable (Babchishin & Helmus 2016, doi:10.3758/s13428-015-0627-7).
